A limited-edition poetry chapbook designed as a speculative archival artifact from a future where sensory experience must be preserved in a museum. Led all design work — cover, interior layout, and typographic system — in close collaboration with the author to build a visual language that reinforces the text's themes of embodiment and alienation.
